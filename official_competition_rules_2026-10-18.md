# Official rules checked for the 2026/27 panel

Checked 18 October 2026 (Europe/Paris page fetch; the match-statistical cutoff remains 2026-10-18 10:54 BRT).

## European access places

The Ligue 1 explainer “2026/27: Everything you need to know about European competitions” says France/Ligue 1 has seven European places in the 2026/27 season. It identifies the top three as direct Champions League league-phase qualifiers; fourth place enters Champions League qualifying; fifth and sixth receive Europa League league-phase places (the sixth place passes from the Coupe de France place because the cup winner already qualified through the league); and seventh can qualify for the Conference League through a two-legged play-off. These are the actual 2026/27 French allocation, not confirmation of the access places for the UEFA season following this panel (2027/28).

Primary: https://ligue1.com/en/articles/l1_article_5609-2026-27-everything-you-need-to-know-about-european-competitions

UEFA's access-list page describes its list as an illustration of clubs that would qualify for the upcoming season, says entry stages follow Annex A of each competition regulation, and explicitly calls the list provisional. At retrieval it displayed “No data available”; it did not provide a verifiable 2027/28 French allocation. Therefore the dashboard keeps Europe markings explicitly provisional and does not extrapolate the 2026/27 French allocation as an official 2027/28 rule.

Primary: https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/accesslist/

## Relegation/play-off

The official LFP 2025/26 article states that the winner of Ligue 2 play-off 2 faces the 16th-placed Ligue 1 club in a two-legged promotion/relegation play-off. It is direct official evidence for the 16th-place playoff in the 2025/26 format, but not an explicit 2026/27 regulation. The specific LFP 2026/27 competition-regulation PDF was not located during this check. Consequently the panel marks the 16th-place playoff and 17th–18th drop-zone display as provisional rather than asserting a confirmed 2026/27 rule.

Primary: https://ligue1.com/fr/articles/l1_article_4680-barrages-25-26-toutes-les-dates
